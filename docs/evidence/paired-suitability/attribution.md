# Additional control photographs

Source metadata was captured during curation, before new model inference. Five photos enter the experiment; the two Fala images are recorded exclusions. Existing corpus attribution remains in [photo-set-v2/attribution.md](../photo-set-v2/attribution.md).

Downloaded Commons renditions are retained under their source licenses. Normalized inputs remove metadata; vision inputs additionally reduce the full frame to a maximum dimension of 1024 pixels without cropping or enlargement. These transformations do not change the source license. The photo licenses are separate from the application license.
