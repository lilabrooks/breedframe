# Response to the methods and direction review

**Closeout, 2026-09-12:** model work is closed for this prototype. Any next-step proposals below are historical and are superseded by the [final findings and stopping decision](findings.md). They are not an active work queue.

Reopen ResNet-50 as a candidate and defer the proposed subject-detection work. The review correctly identifies the reporting-policy confound and an overly broad interpretation of unsuitable inputs. The existing evidence warrants a better controlled comparison, not a promoted threshold or a mandatory two-photo reporting policy.

The owner has now clarified the product rule: cautious visual matches are allowed for real dogs of unknown ancestry; clearly labeled scene-level matches are also allowed when multiple dogs share a breed. An individual-dog assessment still needs a selected subject in a multiple-dog scene. This changes the interpretation of two cohorts, not their recorded observations or ground-truth annotations. Unsupported breeds remain separately reported; this clarification does not establish accuracy for breeds outside a classifier's label space.

The application currently has no report-scope field distinguishing an individual subject from a scene. These semantics are accepted direction for the next experiment, not implemented behavior. A whole-frame breed prediction alone does not establish that every dog shares that breed.

## Findings confirmed

- The maximum leading ViT score found in serialized candidate records under `docs/evidence/`, excluding the ResNet screen, is 0.476691. The v2 maximum is 0.222815. No such recorded score clears the current 0.5 floor. These are stored, repeatedly serialized observations, not a complete audit of every image ever processed or a bound on future scores.
- The direct arm exposes an ungated ranking; rules and agent candidates come from gated reports. Applying the same numerical gate to cached direct initial-photo outputs produces zero reports: 0/2 labeled development cases and 0/4 labeled reserved cases. The report-count differences cannot isolate orchestration value. The README, specification and comparison account have been corrected.
- Whole-photo classification of supplied follow-ups is 0/16 across the four forced-pair records and the CLI/browser demonstrations. The browser later has a separately requested region classification. Rebuilding Sully's post-inspection state confirms that whole-photo classification was allowed, five actions remained, and the missing result was explicitly recorded as a blocker. This is an observed controller choice under the shared configuration; its cause remains unresolved.
- The zero-floor paired replay permits 3/6 supported-dog reports, all correct, and blocks Bo by disagreement. Adding margin 0.15 permits only 1/6. No paired non-dog, multiple-dog or unknown-ancestry evidence exists in this set, so that result has not met the owner's complete reporting criterion. Requiring two photos would also change acquisition burden and the product.
- On the recorded ResNet outputs, multi-02 exceeds every supported photo in both score and margin. Under the original rule that forbids all multiple-dog reports, no pair of minimum score/margin thresholds can admit a supported photo without admitting multi-02. This conditional impossibility is stronger than the original finite-grid result. It was established after inference; the model's outputs were not known when the protocol was frozen.
- With only the non-dog cohort treated as prohibited, ResNet at score 0.8 and margin 0 permits 8/12 supported-photo reports, all correct, and 0/3 non-dog reports. It also permits both multiple-dog photos and the unknown-ancestry photo. Those require the newly approved output semantics; they cannot be silently counted as verified correct individual-breed reports.

## Where the review goes too far

The scoring audit checks implementation consistency on three inputs. It does not establish population calibration quality or the training cause of diffuse scores. Likewise, zero gated reports on recorded inputs does not prove the application can never report on future inputs. The historical v1 also used a different reporting policy and did produce reports.

The skipped follow-ups are meaningful behavioral evidence about these configurations. The gate confounds the final-report metric, but it does not erase observations about actions, requests or resource use. A failure after adding one state field would reject that particular intervention, not prove that the shared input is irrelevant or the controller cannot use state.

One numerical statement in the review is incorrect: a cutoff admitting champ-01 at 0.184269 need not admit the sculpture at 0.180789; 0.182 separates them. The sculpture is fourth, not third, by leading score in the 20-photo ViT set. The broader overlap problem remains: its score and margin exceed several other correctly classified photos.

The crop pilot supports only its three recorded transformations. Buddy's crop retained about 92% of the original width and 83% of its area; two crops were below the classifier's 224-pixel input size, and Bo lacked a supported target. Calling the effective sample size one is not a formal statistical result. The useful correction is that this pilot did not evaluate cropping to separate multiple dogs or remove a dominant foreground person.

ResNet assigning clothing to Conan's first photo is an abstention on a supported live-dog case, not successful non-dog detection. Agreement between models or views is also consistency rather than independent corroboration: they may share training exposure and systematic errors. Reusing exposed subjects for new paired controls can reveal a failure, but determinism does not remove selection bias or make passing results independent validation.

Do not change app thresholds from this reanalysis.
2. **Probe the skipped-follow-up decision with the reporting policy fixed.** Compare the same saved post-inspection development states under the original context and a context that explicitly surfaces current-photo classification status and blockers. Use both pinned controllers, unchanged model settings, schema and budget. Measure classification choices over supplied current-photo opportunities, requests and invalid decisions. This isolates the context intervention before coupling it to a new two-photo rule. Register the bounded schedule and interpretation before inference; a null result rules out only this intervention. Any subsequent deterministic classification policy must be described as a changed division of work.
3. **Compare candidate reporting rules against relevant controls.** ResNet with native scores, ViT cross-view agreement and mapped cross-model agreement are development hypotheses. Before selecting one, define its report scope, freeze the corresponding paired controls and preserve cohort-specific denominators. Include non-dog depictions, unknown ancestry, same-breed and different-breed groups, plus naturally poor/better views if follow-up usefulness is the question. Evaluate cheap failure cases before a larger fresh collection. A passing convenience sample advances to an identity-disjoint evaluation; it does not establish rejection reliability. No detector or larger controller is selected by this review.
